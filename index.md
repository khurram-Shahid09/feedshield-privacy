# FeedShield: Reels Blocker privacy policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

FeedShield is an Android app that closes Reels, Shorts and TikTok. Your privacy is the point of the app.

**What FeedShield collects:** nothing. FeedShield does not collect, store, share or sell personal data.

**Accessibility service.** FeedShield uses Android's Accessibility service only inside Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and
Facebook, to detect when the Reels, Shorts or TikTok screen is open and press Back. It reads screen layout
information (such as view identifiers and the label of the selected tab). It does not read your messages, posts,
passwords or anything you type, and it does not record the screen. Nothing it sees is saved or sent anywhere.

**On your phone only.** Your settings (which apps are blocked, schedule, allowance) and your counts of closed
screens are stored on your device and are deleted when you uninstall FeedShield. Counts older than 30 days are removed
automatically.

**No internet access.** FeedShield does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone.
It has no ads, analytics or crash-reporting services.

**Purchases.** If you buy Pro, the payment is handled by Google Play. FeedShield does not see your payment details.
Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

**Children.** FeedShield is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
